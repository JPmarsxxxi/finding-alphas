# Flow lens — corpus index

**Complete.** Practitioner, academic and primary-source halves all held.

| source | who | what | index |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Donnelly, *The Art of Currency Trading*** (Wiley 2019) | Head of G10 spot, Citi NY -> MD & global head of G10 FX, Nomura NY -> HSBC | 411pp, 11 sections: FX 101, trading the market, risk management, and his 25 rules | `donnelly_art_of_currency_trading/INDEX.md` |
| **Donnelly, *Alpha Trader*** (2021) | as above | 563pp, 16 sections. **ch10 "Understand technicals, sentiment and positioning"** and **ch8 "Understand microstructure"** are the two this lens wants first | `donnelly_alpha_trader/INDEX.md` |
| **Howell, *Capital Wars: The Rise of Global Liquidity*** (Palgrave 2020) | Research Director, Salomon Brothers; Head of Research and board director, Barings; founded CrossBorder Capital 1996 | 316pp, 22 sections. Funding liquidity, cross-border capital flows, the liquidity transmission mechanism, and the Global Liquidity Indexes | `howell_capital_wars/INDEX.md` |
| **Kang, Rouwenhorst & Tang (2020), *A Tale of Two Premiums*** (J. Finance 75:377-417) | Yale SOM / HKU | 61pp. Hedger vs speculator positions and risk premiums in commodity futures — short-run moves driven by noncommercial liquidity demand, long-run by commercial hedging demand, **and the two hit expected returns with opposite signs**. The academic treatment #024 never had | `kang_rouwenhorst_tang_tale_of_two_premiums.txt` |
| **CFTC, *Disaggregated COT Explanatory Notes*** | primary | 4pp. The four categories (Producer/Merchant/Processor/User, Swap Dealers, Managed Money, Other Reportables) and what they do and do not mean | `cftc_disaggregated_cot_explanatory_notes.txt` |
| **CFTC, *Traders in Financial Futures Explanatory Notes*** | primary | 4pp. The financial-futures equivalent breakdown | `cftc_traders_in_financial_futures_notes.txt` |

Together the two CFTC notes are what F5 needs — *"positioning data is stale, partial, and reported by
the wrong people"* is a claim; these documents are the evidence for exactly how.
