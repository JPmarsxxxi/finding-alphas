2.1
Governing equations
V. Bjerknes (1904) pointed out for the ﬁrst time that there is a complete set of seven
equations with seven unknowns that governs the evolution of the atmosphere:
Newton’s second law or conservation of momentum (three equations for the
three velocity components);
the continuity equation or conservation of mass;
the equation of state for ideal gases;
the ﬁrst law of thermodynamics or conservation of energy;
a conservation equation for water mass.
To these equations we have to add appropriate boundary conditions at the bottom
and top of the atmosphere.
In this section we brieﬂy derive the governing equations. The reader may refer to
other texts, such as Haltiner and Williams (1980), or James (1994) for more details.
