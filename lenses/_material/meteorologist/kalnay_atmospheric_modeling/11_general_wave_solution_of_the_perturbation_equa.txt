2.4
Filtering approximations
When we neglect the time derivative of one of the equations of motion, we convert
it from a prognostic equation into a diagnostic equation, and eliminate with it one
type of solution. Physically, we eliminate a restoring force that supports a certain
type of wave. We call this a “ﬁltering approximation”. Use of the quasi-geostrophic
ﬁltering approximation that eliminates both sound and gravity waves made possible
the successful forecast of Charney et al. (1950). Currently most global models and
some regional models use the hydrostatic approximation, which ﬁlters sound waves.
In this section we explore the effect of the ﬁltering approximations.
